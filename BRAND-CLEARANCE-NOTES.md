# Preliminary Brand / IP Clearance Notes

**Working public name:** Remote Job Safety Review  
**Review date:** October 17, 2026  
**Creator:** Daylise Baber

## What was checked

A public-web name check was performed for the exact phrase **“Remote Job Safety Review”**, close descriptive variants, and the phrase combined with software/app/trademark terms. Public USPTO-indexed search results were also checked for obvious exact-match conflicts. No obvious exact-match job-safety software brand or federally indexed mark using the full phrase was surfaced in that preliminary review.

The phrase is highly descriptive. That lowers collision risk for ordinary descriptive use but also makes it a weaker candidate for exclusive trademark protection than a distinctive invented brand.

## Important limitation

This is a **preliminary public clearance review, not a legal trademark opinion**. USPTO guidance explains that comprehensive clearance can involve federal and state trademark databases plus internet/common-law searching, and confusing similarity can exist even when names are not identical. A trademark attorney can perform and interpret a formal clearance search before significant investment or a registration filing.
