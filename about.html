Remote Job Safety Review

About

Remote Job Safety Review helps job seekers slow down, spot common warning signals, understand unclear remote-work terms, and identify what to verify before they share sensitive information or invest more time.

Why it is different

This project is intentionally broader than a simple “scam score.” It separates safety signals from remote-job clarity and gives a concrete next-step checklist. It also avoids pretending that software can prove a company's intent from a pasted message.

Privacy-conscious by design

The first version performs its review in the browser, does not require an account, has no application database, and does not accept uploads. Shareable results omit the pasted message and optional contact fields.

Original visual and content policy

The project uses original written copy, original browser-side code, system fonts, CSS-created interface elements, and a browser-generated share card. It does not use third-party stock photos, copied logos, copied product screenshots, or third-party illustration packs. Government information is paraphrased and credited on the Sources page.
