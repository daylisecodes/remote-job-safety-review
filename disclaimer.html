Remote Job Safety Review

Disclaimer

Remote Job Safety Review is an educational decision-support tool. It is not a fraud determination, employer verification service, or guarantee.

No legitimacy determination

The tool cannot prove that a job, employer, recruiter, website, email, message, or offer is legitimate or fraudulent. Scams can avoid common warning signals, and legitimate employers can sometimes use unusual recruiting practices. Results may include false positives, false negatives, incomplete interpretations, or outdated patterns.

No professional advice

The site does not provide legal, financial, tax, cybersecurity, identity-theft, employment, career-placement, or law-enforcement advice. It is not a substitute for independent research, official guidance, a qualified professional, or direct verification with an employer using contact information you found independently.

Not a background check or consumer report

This site does not conduct background investigations, verify identity, access credit or criminal records, or prepare consumer reports. It is designed for job seekers reviewing opportunities they are considering. It should not be used by employers to screen applicants or make hiring, employment, credit, housing, insurance, or other eligibility decisions about another person.

Government references

The site paraphrases and cites public consumer-safety guidance from agencies including the Federal Trade Commission and the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center. References to those agencies are informational only. Remote Job Safety Review is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or operated by the FTC, FBI, IC3, Vercel, or any employer mentioned by a user.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for deciding whether to apply, communicate, share information, accept an offer, make a payment, report suspected fraud, or take any other action. When money or sensitive information is involved, independently verify the opportunity before acting.
