Privacy

This project is intentionally designed to minimize the amount of information it handles.

Information you enter into the review

The job text, recruiter email, company website, selections, and checklist answers you enter are processed by JavaScript in your browser for the purpose of generating the on-screen review. This version does not send those review inputs to an application database or a server-side analysis endpoint, and it does not create a user account.

Please do not enter highly sensitive information. Remove Social Security numbers, bank-account or routing numbers, passwords, one-time codes, driver's-license or passport numbers, and full home addresses before using the tool.

No file uploads or account storage

This version does not offer resume, screenshot, PDF, or document uploads. It does not use browser localStorage to preserve review inputs after you leave. Clearing or refreshing the page removes the current review from the app interface.

Vercel hosting and Web Analytics

If the live deployment has Vercel Web Analytics enabled, Vercel may process ordinary website traffic and analytics information under its own services and privacy terms. Vercel describes its Web Analytics product as first-party and privacy-friendly and its current privacy notice describes collection of website traffic information such as IP-derived location and system configuration information. This project does not intentionally send your pasted job text, recruiter email, company URL, checklist answers, or result labels as custom analytics events. (Vercel, 2026.)

Read Vercel's current privacy notice at vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice.

External links

The site links to government resources, reporting portals, the creator's portfolio, and potentially other external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy practices. Following a link leaves this site's privacy boundary.

Share and download features

The share-summary and safety-card features are generated from the result summary in your browser. The generated summary is intentionally limited to result labels and the number of flagged signals. It does not include the pasted text or optional contact fields. Your browser or operating system may handle sharing or downloads according to its own settings.
